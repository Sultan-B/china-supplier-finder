Service Scope and Ordering Terms

Last updated: November 2, 2026

These plain-language terms explain the current scope of the China Supplier Finder website. They are service information and do not replace a project-specific written agreement or legal advice.

Before any payment: Obtain a written order confirmation identifying the actual service provider/legal entity, exact deliverables, fee and currency, delivery date, payment method, cancellation and refund terms, and any agreed exclusions. Do not pay until those details are clear.

1. Nature of the service

China Supplier Finder offers public-source desk research intended to help buyers identify and organize potential Chinese supplier candidates. Research may use company websites, public directories, trade-fair pages and other accessible public sources. Reports should identify source links, research dates, company-published claims, information gaps and questions for buyer follow-up where relevant.

2. No supplier verification or guarantee

Unless a separate written agreement expressly states otherwise and the work is actually performed, the service does not include factory visits or audits; authentication of business licences or legal identity; ownership checks; confirmation of factory operations or production capacity; certificate validation; sanctions screening; laboratory testing; product inspection; supplier outreach; quotation collection; negotiation; or transaction management.

Company statements, photographs, certificates or capacity figures found online are not treated as independently verified merely because they appear on a company website. A Public Information Match Score measures only the relevance and completeness of public information against a buyer brief. It is not a supplier reliability, credit, quality, compliance or transaction-risk rating.

3. Buyer responsibilities

Buyers are responsible for independently verifying the supplier's legal identity, contracting entity, factory status, certificates, product specifications, samples, quality, capacity, payment details, shipping arrangements and contractual terms before placing an order or transferring funds. Buyers should obtain professional advice where appropriate.

4. Enquiries, previews and paid work

A request or free-preview application is not an acceptance of work. Preview requests may be reviewed for fit and availability and may be declined. Paid research begins only after the scope, fee, timing and payment arrangements have been confirmed in writing by the service provider and accepted by the buyer.

5. Prices, payment, cancellation and refunds

Prices displayed on the website are introductory test prices and are subject to confirmation for the specific request. This website does not collect payment. The applicable payment method, currency, payment timing, cancellation rights and refund terms must be stated in the written order confirmation before payment. If those terms are not provided, ask for clarification and do not pay.

6. Research limitations

Public information may be incomplete, outdated, inaccurate, unavailable or supplied by the company itself. Research reflects sources accessible and reviewed at the stated research date. The service does not promise that a particular number of suitable suppliers exists or that any candidate will accept an enquiry, meet a specification or complete a transaction.

7. Service provider identity and contact

The public website currently identifies the service by the name “China Supplier Finder” and the contact email tietouwaxs@gmail.com. It does not currently publish a registered legal entity name or postal business address. The actual contracting provider must be identified in writing before a paid order is accepted.

8. Related policy

Information submitted through the website or its embedded questionnaire is addressed in the Privacy Policy. For questions about the service scope, contact tietouwaxs@gmail.com.
