CHINA SUPPLIER FINDER

Service Scope and Ordering Terms

Last updated: November 2, 2026

These plain-language terms explain the current scope of the China Supplier Finder website. They are service information and do not replace a project-specific written agreement or legal advice.

Before any payment: Obtain a written order confirmation identifying the actual service provider/legal entity, exact deliverables, fee and currency, delivery date, payment method, cancellation and refund terms, and any agreed exclusions. Do not pay until those details are clear.

1. Nature of the service

China Supplier Finder offers public-source desk research intended to help buyers identify and organize potential Chinese supplier candidates. Research may use company websites, public directories, trade-fair pages and other accessible public sources. Reports should identify source links, research dates, company-published claims, information gaps and questions for buyer follow-up where relevant.

2. No supplier verification or guarantee

Unless a separate written agreement expressly states otherwise and the work is actually performed, the service does not include factory visits or audits; authentication of business licences or legal identity; ownership checks; confirmation of factory operations or production capacity; certificate validation; sanctions screening; laboratory testing; product inspection; supplier outreach; quotation collection; negotiation; or transaction management.

Company statements, photographs, certificates or capacity figures found online are not treated as independently verified merely because they appear on a company website. A Public Information Match Score measures only the relevance and completeness of public information against a buyer brief. It is not a supplier reliability, credit, quality, compliance or transaction-risk rating.

3. Buyer responsibilities

Buyers are responsible for independently verifying the supplier's legal identity, contracting entity, factory status, certificates, product specifications, samples, quality, capacity, payment details, shipping arrangements and contractual terms before placing an order or transferring funds. Buyers should obtain professional advice where appropriate.

4. Enquiries, previews and paid work

A request or free-preview application is not an acceptance of work. Preview requests may be reviewed for fit and availability and may be declined. Paid research begins only after the scope, fee, timing and payment arrangements have been confirmed in writing by the service provider and accepted by the buyer.

5. Prices, payment, delivery, cancellation and refunds

All package prices displayed on the website are introductory test prices denominated in United States dollars (USD): Basic $10 USD, Standard $25 USD and Premium $50 USD. Prices are subject to written confirmation for the specific project. Any applicable taxes, bank charges, currency-conversion costs or payment-provider fees must be disclosed in the written quote before the buyer agrees. This website does not collect payment.

Written order confirmation. Before payment, the buyer must receive and accept a written order identifying: (a) the actual service provider and contracting identity; (b) the selected package and exact research scope; (c) the target maximum number of supplier records and the deliverable format; (d) the total fee and currency (USD unless expressly agreed otherwise); (e) any applicable taxes or transfer/payment fees; (f) the target delivery date; (g) the payment method and payment schedule; and (h) cancellation and refund terms. Do not pay if these details are missing or unclear.

Supplier quantity and availability. “Up to” 5, 15 or 30 supplier records is a maximum target, not a guarantee that that number of suitable candidates exists. The fee covers the agreed public-source research scope, not a guaranteed number of qualified suppliers. If fewer suitable candidates are identified, we will report the available findings and contact the buyer before materially expanding or changing the search. Any alternative scope, substitute criteria, fee adjustment or cancellation must be agreed in writing. No additional work or fee is authorized by silence.

Delivery timing. There is no automatic delivery-time guarantee based only on the package selected. A target delivery date is confirmed in the project-specific written order after reviewing the product brief, required specifications, target market and search scope. Work does not begin until the order and payment arrangements have been accepted by both parties.

Cancellation and refunds. If the buyer cancels before research work begins, the amount paid for that order will be refunded in full. If the buyer cancels after work has begun, the service provider will identify the work completed and the corresponding amount attributable to completed work; any unearned balance will be refunded. Once the agreed deliverable has been supplied, fees for completed work are not refundable except where required by applicable law or expressly agreed in writing. Any project-specific variation must be disclosed and accepted in the written order before payment. Mandatory consumer rights, where applicable, are not excluded.

6. Research limitations

Public information may be incomplete, outdated, inaccurate, unavailable or supplied by the company itself. Research reflects sources accessible and reviewed at the stated research date. The service does not promise that a particular number of suitable suppliers exists or that any candidate will accept an enquiry, meet a specification or complete a transaction.

7. Service provider identity and contact

The public website currently identifies the service by the name “China Supplier Finder” and the contact email tietouwaxs@gmail.com. It does not currently publish a registered legal entity name or postal business address. This is a material pre-order disclosure: no paid order should be accepted and no payment should be requested until the actual service provider’s lawful contracting identity and the required business/contact details have been confirmed and disclosed to the buyer in writing. The website trading name alone is not a legal-entity representation.

8. Related policy

Information submitted through the website or its embedded questionnaire is addressed in the Privacy Policy. For questions about the service scope, contact tietouwaxs@gmail.com.
