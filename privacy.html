Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy describes the information a visitor may choose to send when enquiring about China Supplier Finder. The website is a static GitHub Pages site; it does not provide customer accounts or an on-site payment checkout. The service provides public-source supplier desk research.

Information you may provide

If you use the Google Forms questionnaire or prepare an email request, you may provide your name, email address, product requirements, target market, specifications and other details you choose to include. Do not submit passwords, payment card details, government identification numbers or other sensitive personal information.

How information is used

Information is used to understand your enquiry, assess the requested research scope, respond to you and, if you agree to proceed, prepare the requested research deliverable.

Third-party services

The questionnaire is embedded from Google Forms and opens on Google's service. If you submit it, the information is handled by Google and may be accessible to the form owner according to the form's configuration. This website does not control Google's data processing, cookies, account requirements, retention or access settings. Review the Google Forms interface and Google's privacy information before submitting. The separate email form only prepares a draft in your own email application; the website does not transmit or receive that draft. You choose whether to send it.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell enquiry information. We do not intentionally share it with unrelated third parties, except where necessary to operate the service through tools you choose to use, with your direction, or where required by law.

Retention and deletion requests

There is no automated deletion function on this static website. Information sent to the service may be kept while it is needed to respond to the enquiry, provide an agreed service, maintain relevant business records or meet legal obligations. No fixed retention period is currently published. To ask about, correct or request deletion of information sent directly to the service, email tietouwaxs@gmail.com. For information submitted through Google Forms, a separate request to the form owner and/or Google may be required.

Security and scope

No method of internet transmission or storage is completely secure. This website does not operate a customer account system or online payment processor. The site may load third-party Google Forms content when the questionnaire is opened. Do not submit passwords, payment details, government identifiers, sensitive personal information or confidential business secrets through the public questionnaire or email form.

Policy updates and contact

This policy may be updated as the service and its third-party tools change. The latest version will be published on this page. The public website currently identifies the service by its trading name and contact email; it does not state a registered legal entity or postal business address. For privacy questions, contact tietouwaxs@gmail.com.
