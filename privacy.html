CHINA SUPPLIER FINDER

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy explains how China Supplier Finder handles information submitted for supplier-research enquiries. The website is hosted as a static site through GitHub Pages and does not currently provide customer accounts, behavioural analytics or an on-site payment checkout. The service provides public-source supplier desk research.

Service identity: The public site currently uses the trading name “China Supplier Finder” and the contact email below. It does not yet publish a registered legal entity name or postal business address. Do not send payment or sensitive personal information through the website. Before any paid order is accepted, the actual contracting provider and required identity/contact details must be disclosed in the written order.

Information you may provide

If you use the Google Forms questionnaire or prepare an email request, you may provide your name, email address, product requirements, target market, specifications and other details you choose to include. Do not submit passwords, payment card details, government identification numbers or other sensitive personal information.

How information is used

Information is used to understand your enquiry, assess the requested research scope, respond to you and, if you agree to proceed, prepare the requested research deliverable.

Website hosting and third-party services

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information such as IP address, browser and request data as part of hosting and security operations under its own policies. This service does not control GitHub’s infrastructure logs or retention practices. The site currently does not intentionally run advertising pixels or third-party behavioural analytics.

The questionnaire is embedded from Google Forms and opens on Google's service. If you submit it, the information is handled by Google and may be accessible to the form owner according to the form's configuration. This website does not control Google's data processing, cookies, account requirements, retention or access settings. Review the Google Forms interface and Google's privacy information before submitting. The separate email form only prepares a draft in your own email application; the website does not transmit or receive that draft. You choose whether to send it.

Data minimization and confidentiality

Provide only information needed to assess the sourcing project. Product requirements and business contact details may be commercially sensitive; do not submit trade secrets, confidential formulas, passwords, identity documents, bank credentials or payment-card details. If confidential information is necessary, ask about an appropriate separate process before sending it.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell enquiry information. We do not intentionally share it with unrelated third parties, except where necessary to operate the service through tools you choose to use, with your direction, or where required by law.

International processing

Google Forms and GitHub Pages are third-party services that may process or store technical or submitted information in jurisdictions outside your own. Review their current terms and privacy notices before using them. The service cannot promise a particular hosting location or cross-border transfer mechanism through this static website.

Retention and deletion requests

There is no automated deletion function on this static website. Information sent to the service may be kept while it is needed to respond to the enquiry, provide an agreed service, maintain relevant business records or meet legal obligations. No fixed retention period is currently published. The service aims to retain enquiry information only for as long as reasonably needed for the enquiry, agreed research, recordkeeping and applicable legal requirements, but an operational retention schedule has not yet been formally established. To ask about, correct or request deletion of information sent directly to the service, email tietouwaxs@gmail.com. For information submitted through Google Forms, a separate request to the form owner and/or Google may be required.

Security and scope

No method of internet transmission or storage is completely secure. This website does not operate a customer account system or online payment processor. The site may load third-party Google Forms content when the questionnaire is opened. Do not submit passwords, payment details, government identifiers, sensitive personal information or confidential business secrets through the public questionnaire or email form.

Cookies and local storage

The website does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies or use browser local storage for customer profiling. Third-party content such as the embedded Google Form may use its own cookies or similar technologies subject to the third party’s settings and policies.

Policy updates and contact

This policy may be updated as the service and its third-party tools change. The latest version will be published on this page. The public website currently identifies the service by its trading name and contact email; it does not state a registered legal entity or postal business address. For privacy questions, contact tietouwaxs@gmail.com.
