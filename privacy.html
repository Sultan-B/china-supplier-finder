Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy explains how China Supplier Finder handles information submitted through this website. The service provides independent supplier desk research.

Information you may provide

If you use the Google Forms questionnaire or prepare an email request, you may provide your name, email address, product requirements, target market, specifications and other details you choose to include. Do not submit passwords, payment card details, government identification numbers or other sensitive personal information.

How information is used

Information is used to understand your enquiry, assess the requested research scope, respond to you and, if you agree to proceed, prepare the requested research deliverable.

Third-party services

The online questionnaire is hosted by Google Forms. Information submitted there is processed through Google's service and is also subject to Google's applicable privacy terms. The email option opens your own email application; this website does not receive or store that email submission.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell enquiry information. We do not intentionally share it with unrelated third parties, except where necessary to operate the service through tools you choose to use, with your direction, or where required by law.

Retention and deletion requests

Enquiry information is retained only for as long as reasonably needed to respond to and manage the enquiry, provide an agreed service, maintain appropriate business records or meet legal obligations. To ask about, correct or request deletion of information submitted to us, email tietouwaxs@gmail.com. Information held by Google may also need to be managed through Google.

Security and scope

No method of internet transmission or storage is completely secure. This website does not operate a customer account system or online payment processor. Do not send confidential business secrets unless you have first agreed an appropriate secure process with us.

Policy updates and contact

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The latest version will be published on this page. For privacy questions, contact tietouwaxs@gmail.com.
