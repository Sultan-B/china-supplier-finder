ABOUT THE SERVICE

Independent supplier desk research

China Supplier Finder helps international buyers organize an initial search for potential suppliers in China.

What we do

We collect and structure information available from public company websites and other accessible public sources, then provide source-linked research records.

What we do not do

We are not a supplier, manufacturer, inspection body, certification agency or legal adviser. We do not conduct factory audits or guarantee supplier claims.

How to use the results

Treat the shortlist as an initial research aid. Contact candidates directly, verify business registration and certifications independently, request samples and complete appropriate checks before ordering.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before you request a search

Are suppliers verified?

No. This is public-source desk research. We do not independently verify company registration, factory ownership, production capacity, certificates or product quality.

How long does research take?

We confirm the expected delivery date after reviewing your product and requirements, before work begins.

How do I pay?

This website does not collect payments. We first review your request and confirm scope, price and payment arrangements directly before starting paid work.

What if information is limited?

Some records may have incomplete public contacts or limited evidence. We note information gaps rather than invent missing details.
